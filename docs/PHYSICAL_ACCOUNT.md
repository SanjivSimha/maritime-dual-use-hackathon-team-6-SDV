# Physical Account

ShipOS is a resource governor prototype, checked here against the code on `main` at `ffe6f3f`.
Every number below is an Annex A/B value or an entry in the assumption registry. The registry,
with each entry's file:line uses, is [PHYSICAL_ACCOUNT_REGISTRY.md](PHYSICAL_ACCOUNT_REGISTRY.md),
generated by `python -m tools.physical_account`. This is a simplified engineering model: it is not
certified, has not been tried on a ship, and is not integrated with real cameras or sensors.

## What is calculated (implemented, `shipos/physics`, `shipos/engine/kernel.py`)

| Class | Model |
|---|---|
| Power | Capacity is the **reserve available to guests**, not generator rating: 200 kW with both gensets, 100 kW with one (A.1 at cruise; stopped / slow / fast reserves are assumptions). A spinning reserve (registry: 0 kW) is withheld with a genset down. Peaks: +250 / +100 kW for ≤ 60 s. Two 150 kW mission lines; the line lost on a trip is an assumption. Demand is each mode's declared `power_kw` (or a measured value in the 1 Hz gauges). |
| Heat | Loop capacity falls linearly from 160 kW at 12 °C to 120 kW at 30 °C, is clamped to those values outside 12–30 °C, and is × 0.6 when degraded (72 kW at 30 °C). Electrical demand of **water-cooled** guests becomes loop heat 1:1; **air-cooled** guests (G1 on the mast) add none. A +30 kW / 60 s inertia token exists in `physics/cooling.py` but is not used by any decision yet. |
| Mass | Raw total vs the 10 t margin. "Stability" is a **proxy**: Σ mass × mounting multiplier (bays 1.0, hangar and roof 1.2, mast 3.0) against 12 t-equivalent. No GM, VCG, free-surface or hydrostatic calculation. |
| Deck / mast | Slot groups and units (Bay 1 whole or two halves, Bay 2, hangar, roof, three mast positions of which two hold ship equipment). |
| Data | SATCOM for admission is the worst case: 2 Mbit/s × 70 % − 0.5 Mbit/s ship reserve = 0.9 Mbit/s. SATCOM and radio are 0 in silence; acoustic is 0 with manoeuvring, deck machinery or layered water. Elastic demands are reported as lost capability, not refusal. |
| Compute | 80 % of the rack: ⌊4 × 32 × 0.8⌋ = 102 cores, 409.6 GB RAM, ⌊4 × 1 × 0.8⌋ = 3 **whole** accelerators. |
| Time | Decision ceilings from A.8. The transition planner (`statemachine.plan_transition`) uses A.6 upper bounds. Speed, course and noise settle **concurrently**, with two explicit dependencies: an acoustic hold must arrive (≤ 60 s) before any manoeuvre, and human confirmation follows deck clearing. Example from its tests: G4 recovery from G3's survey state is ready at 360 s with 14 crew and 900 s with 6. Durations are not summed indiscriminately. |

D1 admission checks every envelope corner (speed band × gensets online at the hottest seawater)
for the worst simultaneous mode combination. D2 placement maximises the minimum normalised margin.
Both are implemented. **D3 arbitration and D4 shedding are not**: both raise
`NotImplementedError` on `ffe6f3f`.

## Figures that are easy to get wrong

- **G3 includes its cradle.** `surveying`, `holding` and `recovering` are 1 kW of ship load; only
  `charging` is 25 kW.
- **Scenario B at minute 12 shows no overload yet.** No Scenario B timeline is on `main`. In the
  open simulator PR the genset trips at t = 720 s with G1 on, G3 surveying, G4 flying and G5 on:
  1.5 + 1 + 2 + 45 = 49.5 kW at card values, well under the 100 kW one-genset reserve. The
  106.5 kW case (G4 charging 35 + G3 charging 25 + G5 45 + G1 1.5) needs both charges to overlap.
  No run has produced that overlap yet.
- **+40 kW of cooling is a proposal.** A loop rated 160 kW at 30 °C, from the planning analysis
  that heat binds before power in warm water. It is not a measured or optimised result.

## Requirements vs. assumptions vs. unverified behaviour

- **Requirements (Annex):** A.1–A.11 limits, Annex B cards for G1–G5, A.8 decision ceilings.
- **Assumptions:** 64 registry entries, each with value, unit, range, reason and source. Eight are
  not read as numbers anywhere: they record modelling choices the code makes structurally (for
  example "plan on upper bounds" and "transitions concurrent"). The appendix lists them as unused
  rather than inventing uses for them.
- **Unverified integrated behaviour:** the simulator, API/UI and crew policy are in open PRs. The
  crew policy's deck "camera" evidence is simulated. End-to-end Scenario A/B runs, D3/D4
  outcomes and decision-log determinism across the integrated system have not been demonstrated.

## Material limitations

Steady-state power and heat with declared, not measured, card values. No transient electrical or
thermal model beyond the unused inertia token. Static worst-case link rates with no weather or
geometry. Stability is a weighted mass proxy, not naval architecture. Transition times are A.6
upper bounds, not a manoeuvring model. Validation is limited to unit and golden tests derived
from Annex inputs.
